First Mission Flow and UI Improvements

Wild Boar Forest flow reworked with a unified interface style

The Wild Boar Forest escort now advances naturally, while following and intercepting are controlled directly by the player. This update also unifies the main UI and improves startup presentation.

This update focuses on the first mission, “Wild Boar Forest.” The escort party now advances on its own instead of waiting for mission buttons. If Lu Zhishen does not follow, he will miss the rescue as the story continues. Following and intercepting are now handled directly by the player. After Lin Chong is saved, unbinding him, treating his injuries, and leaving the forest continue as part of the scene.

We have also unified the visual style across the main menu, battle interface, settings, hero codex, and editors. The startup image has been replaced, and icon and resource caching at launch have been improved.

This build is currently intended for testing across multiple devices. If you encounter a progression block, display issue, or awkward control, please let us know which mission you were playing and what you were doing.
