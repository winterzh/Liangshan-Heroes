Windows test update: directional combat and death visuals

Four common government troop types now have walking, attack, and falling animations for all four directions. The archer now faces and lines up correctly when moving southwest, and the cavalry's spear stance and thrust are clearer.

We also reduced the size of blood marks and dropped equipment. They fade in shortly after the body starts falling. The hit flash no longer stays on a dead unit, and the shadow fades with the body.

This update is intended for cross-device testing on Windows. It does not mean that art for the full roster is complete, does not include a dedicated 30-wave performance validation, and is not a full release.
