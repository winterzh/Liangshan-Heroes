Title
I saw that suggestion, and I've been working on it

Summary
More directions for characters, clearer shadows, better terrain, and battlefields that do not look untouched. I have been working through them bit by bit.

Body
A few days ago, a player left some useful feedback in the discussions: characters needed more facing directions, shadows were hard to see, terrain looked too flat, and battlefields stayed too clean after a fight.

I saw it, and it was specific enough to work from. So that is what I've been doing.

I'm still adding four-direction animations. I redid the unit and building shadows, and I'm continuing to work on forests, shorelines, docks, and several campaign maps. I have a first version of persistent battle marks working, but it still needs testing.

September 5 is close. Anything that tests well will make the release build. Anything that is not ready will wait.

Here are a few images from the current work. The first is key art; the next two show work-in-progress direction sprites and battle remains. They are not gameplay screenshots, and the final result still depends on testing. You can find gameplay images on the store page.
