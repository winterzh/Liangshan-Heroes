## 英文正文

**September 7 Update: Gameplay Improvements and Fixes**

概述：Added a reduced effects option, improved mission and control hints, and fixed settings panel and throwing axe issues.

- Added a reduced effects option to decrease decorative particles and trails.
- Improved control hints in Wild Boar Forest, mission information in Zhu Family Village, and unit selection after rescues.
- Fixed settings panel layering and closing issues.
- Fixed throwing axes failing to process later hits when a target became invalid, and reduced some redundant drawing calculations.
