# 首批工坊示例作品文案候选

两份作品取自游戏内 `WorkshopExamples`。地图示例已在 Steam 客户端实机完成两波并获胜，使用实机封面与下列双语说明上传为作品 `3806632089`；据守示例于 2026-09-24 上传为公开作品 `3807085508`，首波准备期由 10 秒调整为 90 秒，保留初始资源、原生单位数值和两波共 13 名敌人。地图的发布与订阅实战收据见 [`qa/workshop_public_20260923/`](../../qa/workshop_public_20260923/README.md)。

## 地图：示例地图 · 水泊练兵

**简体中文说明：** 在水泊营寨守住聚义厅，击退两波官军。初始有宋江、林冲和聚义厅；敌军从东侧进攻。可以用场景编辑器修改地形、部署和波次。本示例不计入 Steam 成就。

**English description:** Defend the Liangshan assembly hall against two small waves of government troops. Start with Song Jiang and Lin Chong while enemies approach from the east. Open the scenario editor to change terrain, deployment, and waves. Workshop play does not count toward Steam achievements.

## 据守：示例据守 · 两波练兵

**简体中文说明：** 用 250 金、150 木和 90 秒准备期，招募英雄并守住忠义堂。击退两波共 13 名官军：先迎战刀盾兵，再面对刀盾兵与弓手。首次建议关闭倍率、招募林冲守住东侧，可使用游戏内英雄托管。可在据守编辑器调整单位和波次。本示例不计入 Steam 成就。

**English description:** Start with 250 gold, 150 wood and 90 seconds to recruit heroes and defend the hall. Defeat two waves of 13 enemies in total: swordsmen first, then swordsmen and archers. For a first attempt, disable stat scaling and recruit Lin Chong to guard the eastern approach; the in-game hero assistance option is available. Edit units and waves in the defense editor. Workshop play does not count toward Steam achievements.

两份封面分别使用实机截图 `sample_scenario_cover.jpg` 与 `sample_defense_cover.jpg`。据守公开链接：https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3807085508 。公开 API 与订阅文件校验见 `qa/workshop_defense_public_20260924/`。
